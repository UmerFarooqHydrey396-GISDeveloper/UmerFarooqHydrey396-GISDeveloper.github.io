# Umer Farooq Hydrey · GIS Portfolio

**Live site:** https://umerfarooqhydrey396-gisdeveloper.github.io

Portfolio of Umer Farooq Hydrey, GIS Manager & Photogrammetric Analyst. It covers drone photogrammetry,
cadastral QA and land-records integration, remote sensing, and hazard/habitat mapping projects.

## Featured case studies

- Cadastral QA & PLRA Green Property integration
- Multi-district drone mapping & photogrammetry
- Cadastral QA & official approval of 268 mouzas
- Abu Dhabi arid habitat mapping & LULC analysis
- Deep learning building extraction & UAV surveys
- Flood susceptibility & settlement damage analysis
- 31-year cryosphere snow-cover change detection
- Morphometric landslide & flood hazard modeling
- Kunhar Basin hydrology & hotel site suitability

## Structure

